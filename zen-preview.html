Summary of Changes

1. Language — All terminal jargon replaced with human words. SCREAMING_CAPS become sentence case. Machine commands become gentle invitations.
2. Accent color — #D71921 (fire-engine red) shifts to #B5443E (aged persimmon). Still warm, still magnetic, no longer alarming.
3. Background — #030303 (void) warms to #1A1A18 (ink). Dot-matrix, film grain, and scanner line removed. Crowd photo + overlay + vignette remain.
4. Effects — Neon glows, pulse wires, tech brackets, sonar ripples, and interference pulses all removed. Replaced with subtle opacity transitions and gentle breathing indicators.
5. Dialog — AlertTriangle becomes a checkmark. "Finalize Protocol?" becomes "Confirm your booking". ABORT/EXECUTE becomes Back/Confirm.
6. Numbers — Zero-padded "02 SOULS" becomes just "2". The customer chose a number, not a register value.
7. Success — "Booking Confirmed." becomes "See you there." — warm, personal, memorable. The Rei (bow).

This is a static preview. No source code has been modified.
